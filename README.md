👋 Hi, I’m @martinatjie

🌴 Welcome to my coding jungle! My projects grow from all the things I love - arts and crafts, games, design, mental health, neurodiversity, clever tools, and even baking. You’ll find a mix of ideas here, all inspired by the colorful things that make life exciting.

🌱 During the COVID pandemic, I realized how important it is to stay connected. Many of my projects and ideas focus on bringing people together and making life a bit easier for everyone.

🛠 My coding style:

- **Fix-It Focused**: I love solving problems and making life simpler with smart, practical solutions.
- **People First**: My apps aim to be friendly, fun, and easy to use — tech that helps people, not confuses them.
- **Creative Explorer**: From adventure-writing apps to games, I enjoy trying new things and letting my imagination run wild.
- **Time-Saving Pro**: I like building tools that do the hard work for you, saving time and effort.
- **Big Picture, Tiny Details**: I dream big but always take care of the small stuff, too.

🎒 I’m always learning something new because I can’t help myself! I started with C#/.NET, but I love exploring other technologies to grow my skills.

🦜 Let’s team up! I’d love to work on projects filled with passion, especially those that make life better or help other self-taught developers—especially women in tech like me.
